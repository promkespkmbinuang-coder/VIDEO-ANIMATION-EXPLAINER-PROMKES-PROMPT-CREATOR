Check: (1) one MCG, seven unique Guides, three protocols and resolved refs; (2) one owner per Guide, S02 health truth, S13 index/GUIDE-07 only; (3) no broken nodes, duplicate IDs, self-edges, cycles/deadlocks; (4) immutable/controlled/scene-dependent state rules; (5) S13 post-storyboard refresh before S08, with still/motion separated; (6) health claims resolve to S02 and preserve evidence/provenance; (7) provider/model/version capability evidence; (8) asset lifecycle, provenance, rights/license, privacy and consent; (9) LOCKED approval bound to exact artifact hash and human authority.

Every PASS requires test ID, oracle, expected/observed result, environment, timestamp and evidence. NOT_RUN, NOT_VERIFIED, BLOCKED, STALE and INVALIDATED are not PASS. Schema validation does not prove runtime, media QA or human authorization.
