## Architecture decisions

1. S11 remains the only owner of social-distribution artifacts; no S15, parallel autonomous platform skills, scheduler or new production gate is introduced.
2. Five internal S11 lanes are used: Editorial Intelligence; Format Engineering; Platform Intelligence & Planning; Community & Service Interaction; Repurposing & Learning.
3. The 18 Instagram-derived capabilities are classified as 1 ADOPTABLE, 8 PARTIAL and 9 ABSENT. The Promkes beat-sheet/motion contracts are reused rather than replaced with Instagram code.
4. Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube have separate discovery-surface models, formats, diagnostics and eligibility/originality safeguards. No universal algorithm score or secret ranking weights are claimed.
5. S02 retains exclusive health-claim authority; S03 campaign intent and CTA; S04 canonical narrative/VO; S13 continuity; S12 independent QA; M00/S14 orchestration, dependency and release preflight.
6. Community, Stories, DMs, metrics and content logs use consent, minimum-data, human escalation, rights and publication-authorization controls.
7. Existing 45 AT + 13 REF cases and the canonical 58-case traceability inventory remain unchanged. The new 34 social scenarios and traceability sidecar are separate.
