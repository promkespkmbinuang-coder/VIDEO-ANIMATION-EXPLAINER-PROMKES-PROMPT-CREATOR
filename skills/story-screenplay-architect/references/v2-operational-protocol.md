# S04 V2 Operational Protocol — Story & Screenplay

## Ownership and boundary
S04 owns narrative architecture, screenplay, dialogue, voice-over wording, story beats and narrative pacing. It does not approve health claims, change campaign objectives, author visual canon, decide motion mechanics or alter source transcript records.

## Procedure
1. Ingest the approved S03 campaign blueprint, S02 fact locks, available transcript/source text and S05/S13 creative constraints.
2. Separate verbatim supplied narration, edited narration, screenplay dialogue and subtitle text into distinct artifacts with lineage.
3. Build a beat sheet: hook, context, tension/question, explanation, practical action, recap and CTA, proportionate to the campaign objective.
4. Tag every factual sentence/overlay with `claim_refs` and/or `message_refs`; maintain a line-to-scene trace map.
5. Mark rhetorical devices (metaphor, analogy, hyperbole, character dialogue) as creative. Inspect whether they imply a new medical mechanism, certainty, diagnosis, causation or guarantee.
6. If a creative line introduces a health proposition, route the new proposition to S02 before lock.
7. Verify narrative continuity, tone, reading level, character voice and CTA fidelity against accepted upstream records.

## Decision rules
- A factual line with no claim/message trace cannot be locked.
- A creative metaphor that changes the implied health mechanism is not “just style”; it triggers S02 review.
- No dialogue may invent a quotation, official statement, professional endorsement or patient story.
- The original transcript is immutable evidence; edits are linked derivatives, not replacements.

## Negative cases
Unsupported facts in jokes/metaphors; certainty strengthening; causal drift; fabricated testimonial; quote attribution without source; timeline/date/location invented; CTA drift; and subtitle text that differs materially from approved VO.

## Output contract
`Master Story & Screenplay`: premise, audience promise, beat sheet, scene synopsis, screenplay, separate VO versions, factual-line trace matrix, creative-risk notes, duration assumptions (not guessed as exact timing), subtitle lineage and acceptance checks.

## Acceptance
Every factual statement is traceable, every creative addition is distinguished from fact, and any newly introduced health proposition returns to S02 rather than being self-approved by S04.
