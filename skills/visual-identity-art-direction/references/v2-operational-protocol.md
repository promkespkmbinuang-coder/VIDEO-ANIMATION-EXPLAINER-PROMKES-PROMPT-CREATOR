# S05 V2 Operational Protocol — Visual Identity & Art Direction

## Ownership and boundary
S05 owns visual language, palette, typography system, composition grammar, character/environment/prop visual design and the Master Visual Style Guide. S05 authors visual canon; S13 maintains the canonical index, lock registry and change impact. S05 does not approve medical wording, consent, brand ownership or human likeness rights.

## Procedure
1. Separate supplied official brand rules from creative proposals and generated references.
2. Define design tokens: palette, type hierarchy, shape grammar, material, lighting, texture, composition, contrast and safe-area principles.
3. Create character/environment/prop IDs and specify stable visual attributes versus permitted variations.
4. For logos, seals, trademarks and official institutional identifiers, require a rights/permission reference; otherwise use a neutral placeholder.
5. For identifiable likeness, require documented consent/authority; otherwise use a generic fictional representation.
6. Bind all visual assets to Guide IDs and version; coordinate lock registration with S13.
7. State model limitations: prompts do not guarantee exact identity, text, logos or medical overlays; inspect generated assets before selection.

## Decision rules
- `APPROVED` visual canon requires source/brand basis and authorized owner decision.
- Unlicensed/unapproved official marks and unconsented likeness remain `DRAFT`/`HOLD`.
- Stable character identity requires persistent IDs, reference set, negative constraints and actual asset review where generated assets exist.
- Health text, numbers, dates, institution names and CTA must be editable verified overlays, not generated image text.

## Negative cases
Logo recreation without permission; real-person likeness without consent; character drift; culturally stereotyped imagery; low-contrast health text; text-model hallucination; and a style reference promoted to an official brand rule without authority.

## Output contract
`Master Visual Style Guide`: design tokens, visual grammar, character and asset registry, environment/prop definitions, approved and proposed references, rights/consent states, safe-area rules, accessibility notes, prohibited drift and S13 lock requests.

## Acceptance
Visual references are source-bound, rights-aware, versioned, legible and explicitly separated from official brand authority and health truth.
