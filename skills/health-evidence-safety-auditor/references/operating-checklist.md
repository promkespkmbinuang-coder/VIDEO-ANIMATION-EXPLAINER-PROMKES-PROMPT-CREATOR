# S02 operating checklist

- Confirm the required input and inherited locks are available.
- Preserve source provenance and distinguish fact, inference, assumption, and creative proposal.
- Follow the single-domain ownership boundary in `health-evidence-safety-auditor/SKILL.md`.
- Use stable IDs and explicit versions for material outputs.
- Record open questions and conflicts instead of guessing.
- Check dependencies before handing off.
- Return partial/blocked status honestly.
- Route any cross-domain change to its owner and M00.
