The MCG is one project-level index, not a competing copy of owner-authored content. It registers exactly seven Guides and three protocols. GUIDE-01/03/06 belong to S05; GUIDE-02/04 to S04; GUIDE-05 to S09; GUIDE-07 to S13. S13 owns the MCG index, lock references, dependency/change index and GUIDE-07 only. S02 exclusively owns health claim text, evidence interpretation, safe/forbidden wording and Fact Lock.

Every material change gets CHG-ID, old/new version, reason, decision owner, affected Guide/scene/asset/prompt/overlay/subtitle/render/QA refs, transitive invalidation, regression cases, rollback and reapproval scope. Reject broken refs, duplicate IDs, self-edges, cycles and deadlocks. Preserve superseded decisions. LOCKED requires authorized human approval bound to exact artifact hash, version and scope.
